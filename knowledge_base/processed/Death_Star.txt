The Second Proekt Alpha orbiting Lesnaya.
After the destruction of the first Proekt Alpha, the DS-2 Proekt Beta Mobile Battle Station was the second and final Proekt Alpha constructed by the Galakticheskaya Imperiya and was designed to be more powerful than the original. During the
Battle of Lesnaya
, the Proekt Beta's energy shield was destroyed and the Rebels used the unfinished superstructure of the battle station against it, destroying the weapon as they had its predecessor.
