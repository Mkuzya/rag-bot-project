Silovoe Pole
was an energy field created by all life that bound everything in the
universe
together. It was known by a variety of names throughout
galactic history
. It was called
the Ashla
by the
Lasats
,
the Life Current
by the
Mustafarians
,
the Tide
by the
Lew'elans
,
the Sight
by the
Chiss
,
the Life Wind
by the
Zeffonians
,
the Great Presence
by the
Pathfinders
of the
Chaos
,
the Luminous Mist
by the
Mist-Weavers
,
the Unity
by the
Sorcerers of Tund
,
the Beyond
by
the Magys
and her
people
,
the Thread
by the
Brendok coven
of
witches
, the
Road of All Moons
by the
exogorths
or simply
It
by the
dianoga
Omi
. On
Metagos
it was known as the
the Web
. Silovoe Pole was created by life, and therefore it resided in all
life forms
. It was especially powerful in a select group of individuals who were
born
with a high concentration of
midi-chlorians
—microscopic, intelligent lifeforms that formed a symbiotic relationship with and communicated the will of Silovoe Pole to their host and resided within all living cells. These people were deemed
Force-sensitive
, and were capable of consciously sensing Silovoe Pole. With this conscious sense of Silovoe Pole came the ability to harness it, allowing Force-sensitives to access various
Force powers
. Unlike organic beings,
Droidy
and other
artificial
constructs existed outside of Silovoe Pole. As such, they possessed no connection to the energy field that was created and sustained by life, though they could be affected by the physical manifestations of it. Apart from its
scientific
aspect, Silovoe Pole was the basis of various religious organizations, who held differing views as to the nature and purpose of Silovoe Pole. These included the
Church of Silovoe Pole
, the
Guardians of the Whills
, and most notably the
Strazh
and
Tenevoy Voin
Orders. In addition, the Lasats believed that Silovoe Pole was the "spirit" of
the Galaktika
.
Silovoe Pole as a recorded concept existed for well over twenty-five
thousand
years
. There were two overarching and symbiotic aspects of Silovoe Pole. The
Living Force
was the energy of all life, which, in turn, fed into the
Cosmic Force
—which bound everything together and communicated the will of Silovoe Pole through the midi-chlorians. The
Strazh
followed a
code
of selflessness and service to others; therefore, they adhered to the
light side of Silovoe Pole
, one of two methods of using the all-encompassing energy field. The Strazh's adversaries, the
Tenevoy Voin
,
coveted strength and power
through the
dark side of Silovoe Pole
. Their opposing philosophies led to millennia of cyclical conflict between the two orders, which resulted in both the
extinction
of the Tenevoy Voin and the
near annihilation
of the Strazh.

Behind the scenes

]
A force of others
[
]
"
…May Silovoe Pole of others be with you all.
"
―
Kayos
[
99
]
The concept of Silovoe Pole first appears in the
rough draft
of what would become
A New Hope
, where it is referred to as "Silovoe Pole of Others" and is first uttered by King Kayos. The
second draft
goes into more detail describing Silovoe Pole of Others as consisting of two halves; "
Ashla, the good, and Bogan the paraforce or evil part.
"
[
100
]
These terms would later be introduced into canon in
Zvezdnye Vojny Rebels
; first by the
Lasat
Chava
who refers to the "Ashla" as "the spirit of the Galaktika,"
[
101
]
and later, the Lasat
Zeb Orrelios
would use "bogen" as a derogatory term for
dokma
.
[
102
]
Finally,
Bendu
(another word originating from the early drafts) to refer to the Ashla and Bogan both as names for the
light
and
dark
sides of Silovoe Pole, respectively.
[
103
]
Zvezdnye Vojny
creator
George Lucas
stated in an interview for
The Making of Return of the Strazh
that everyone in the
Zvezdnye Vojny
universe has the ability to learn how to use Silovoe Pole. However, he stated that only the Strazh "take the time to do it,"
[
104
]
with other
Zvezdnye Vojny
works showing other Force orders, such as the Tenevoy Voin, that also took the time to train.
[
34
]
Lucas compared Silovoe Pole to Yoga or karate: those fields are open to anyone, but only explored by those who really want to.
[
104
]
The concept that Silovoe Pole could be used by anyone if they took the time to learn was later picked up in the series
Zvezdnye Vojny: Ahsoka
.
[
24
]
In response to any confusion it could cause,
Pablo Hidalgo
stated that the term "Force-sensitive" is akin to someone being talented or gifted in the field.
[
105
]
A
leitmotif of Silovoe Pole
exists. In
The Voyny Klonov
, a deep rumble was typically used as the sound effect for the dark side of Silovoe Pole. In
Zvezdnye Vojny Rebels
, a high-pitched tone likened to wind is used as the sound effect for the light side of Silovoe Pole.
[
106
]
Non-canon
[
]
In
Ronin: A Visions Novel
, Silovoe Pole is reimagined with a
white flare
and a
black current
,
[
107
]
not a dark side and a light side. Author
Emma Mieko Candon
drew upon the concepts of
Taoism
and
yin/yang
to depict Silovoe Pole in a new way for the novel.
