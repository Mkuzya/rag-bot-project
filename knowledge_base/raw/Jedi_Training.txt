Force-sensitive beings were brought to the Jedi Temple on Coruscant, where they learned the ways of the Force as members of the Jedi Order.
Jedi training
was the action of teaching an apprentice the ways of
the Force
in the
Jedi Order
. Under the
Galactic Republic
,
Force-sensitive
beings
were brought to the
Jedi Temple
on
Coruscant
from across
the galaxy
and trained as
Jedi younglings
by
Grand Master
Yoda
. The
younglings
advanced to the next stage of their training as the
Padawan
of either a
Jedi Master
or the lesser ranked
Jedi Knight
. If they passed the
Jedi Trials
, the Padawan would ascend to the full status of a Jedi Knight, and therefore, be considered a full member of the Jedi Order.
With the fall of the Jedi Order during
Order 66
, Jedi training was an all but forgotten art, although it ultimately endured through the few survivors that escaped the
Great Jedi Purge
. After the destruction of the
Sith
,
Luke Skywalker
attempted to restore the Jedi Order by training a new generation of Jedi. Although his plans were ruined by
Ben Solo
's fall to the
dark side of the Force
, Skywalker passed his knowledge on to a final apprentice, a
scavenger
from
Jakku
known as
Rey
.
Overview
[
]
"
When I was out there, alone, all I had was your training and the lessons you taught me. And because of you, I did survive. And not only that, I was able to lead others to survive as well.
"
―Ahsoka Tano, to Anakin Skywalker
[
2
]
A Jedi must resist the temptation of the dark side lest they be consumed by it.
Members of the
Jedi Order
trained in the ways of
the Force
.
[
3
]
The training of each
Jedi
varied. depending on their Master. Masters' styles ranged from the maverick
Qui-Gon Jinn
,
[
4
]
to the emotionally driven
Anakin Skywalker
,
[
5
]
to more traditional Jedi such as
Obi-Wan Kenobi
,
[
6
]
Mace Windu
,
[
7
]
and
Grand Master
Yoda
.
[
8
]
Subjects taught included advanced tuition in other disciplies such as: military history,
[
9
]
diplomacy, science,
[
10
]
investigation,
[
11
]
ancient history,
[
12
]
and medical science.
[
13
]
Dueling was widely considered to be the best training for Padawans, although it was fairly impractical since Jedi were the only ones wielding lightsabers during the Republic.
[
12
]
They were also trained in resistance to mind probes and interrogation.
[
14
]
During the
Clone Wars
, Padawans were given additional training to make competent
commanders of the Republic military
under a
Jedi General
due to the
Galactic Republic
's lack of military leaders.
[
15
]
Overall, every Jedi was duty-bound to observe the tenets of the
Jedi Code
, one of which forbade the Jedi from training more than one apprentice at a
time
. As Jedi, they were trained to rely only on the
light side
for power, to disregard accolades and resist other temptations of the
dark side of the Force
.

History
[
]
Age of the Jedi
[
]
"
Anakin, training to be a Jedi isn't an easy challenge. And even if you succeed, it's a hard life.
"
―Qui-Gon Jinn, to Anakin Skywalker
[
16
]
As Grand Master of the Jedi Order, Yoda personally oversaw the younglings' training in the Jedi Temple on Coruscant.
During the
reign
of the
Galactic Republic
,
Force-sensitive
beings
were identified by their
midi-chlorian
levels and ultimately taken to the
Core World
Coruscant
to begin their training in the
Jedi Temple
.
[
16
]
Training began at the level of the Jedi younglings, who studied in clans consisting of twenty initiates. Together, the
younglings
were taken to the distant
world
of
Ilum
where they could acquire a
kyber crystal
as part of a rite of passage known as
the Gathering
, after which they would craft their
lightsaber
using the
crystal
as its power source.
[
18
]
Following the
Initiate Trials
, a youngling had to be chosen as an apprentice, formally known as
Padawan
, in order to advance to the next stage in their path to becoming a full member of the Jedi Order.
[
19
]
Those who held the rank of
Jedi Knight
were tasked with mentoring the next generation of Jedi, while the elder members, known as
Jedi Masters
, were also free to select a Padawan at their leisure.
[
20
]
After further honing their skills in
lightsaber combat
and various
Force techniques
, a Padawan could undertake the
Jedi Trials
[
19
]
once deemed ready by the
Jedi High Council
. For those who passed their final tests, the title of Jedi Knight was conferred upon them by the
Council
.
[
16
]
Dark times
[
]
"
There's nothing for me here now. I want to learn the ways of the Force and become a Jedi like my father.
"
―Luke Skywalker, to Obi-Wan Kenobi
[
21
]
The training of Luke Skywalker represented a new hope for the Jedi Order, which was all but destroyed by the Galactic Empire.
In the aftermath of
Order 66
, the Jedi Order was all but extinct, reduced to a handful of survivors and fugitives hiding from the newly risen
Galactic Empire
. The Jedi that the Empire
captured
were subjected to
torture
and
indoctrination
, causing them to fall to the dark side and become members of the
Inquisitorius
. However, a few Jedi managed to survive the
Great Jedi Purge
, such as
Cal Kestis
, who served as the Padawan of
Jedi General
Jaro Tapal
during the
Clone Wars
. After befriending another former Jedi,
Cere Junda
, Kestis set out to
rebuild
his Order by finding Force-sensitive children whom he could train as the next generation of Jedi. However, due to the threat that the Empire posed to their safety, Kestis ultimately abandoned this mission, believing the children's destinies should be trusted to the Force. But over the course of his journey, Kestis was able to relearn the Jedi arts and complete his training, by which time he was knighted by Junda, who also reconnected with her Jedi heritage.
[
3
]
Kanan Jarrus
, another former Padawan who survived Order 66, found his own student:
Ezra Bridger
, an orphan whose latent Force-sensitivity made him a candidate for Jedi training.
[
22
]
Despite his initial attempts to train Bridger, Jarrus did not feel suitable as a mentor, having never completed his own training. He set out to
rescue
Jedi Master
Luminara Unduli
after discovering that she might have survived the Purge, believing she would be a better teacher for Bridger. Ultimately, the rumor was a trap to lure fugitive Jedi to
the Grand Inquisitor
.
[
23
]
Jarrus redoubled his efforts to properly train Bridger, but at one point his apprentice demonstrated potential with the dark side. Concerned by Bridger's ability to harness the dark side, Jarrus warned him to not lose control of his emotions.
[
24
]
Although he was reaching the age of nine hundred, Yoda worked to train Luke Skywalker on Dagobah.
Approximately two
decades
after the
Galactic
Empire
rose to power
,
Luke Skywalker
embarked on a journey to become a Jedi Knight like his
father
before him. He was initially trained by Anakin Skywalker's former Master,
Obi-Wan Kenobi
, who taught Luke how to sense the Force through his feelings. However, their time together as Master and apprentice was cut short by Kenobi's
death
at the hands of the
Sith Lord
Darth
Vader,
[
21
]
forcing the young Skywalker to continue his Jedi training alone. After communing with the
Force spirit
of Kenobi, Skywalker traveled to
Dagobah
, where he discovered Yoda, the ancient Grand Master of the Jedi Order.
[
1
]
But Yoda was initially discouraged by Skywalker's attitude, seeing much of Anakin's impatience and anger in Luke. After some consideration and Kenobi's endorsement, Yoda chose to overlook Skywalker's age as well as his penchant for adventure and excitement in order to complete his training.
[
8
]
Skywalker and Yoda were often at odds throughout their time together, with the latter trying to teach his apprentice to be calm and in control of his developing Jedi abilities. Yet Skywalker remained reckless, cutting short his training to
confront
Darth Vader. As a result, he suffered an agonizing defeat in battle against the
Dark Lord of the Sith
, who revealed his former identity as the boy's father, Anakin Skywalker. By the time Luke returned to Dagobah to finish his Jedi training, Yoda had become ill and was on the verge of becoming one with the Force. Yoda told Skywalker that, while he required no further formal training, he had to confront his father one last time in order to finally become a true Jedi.
[
8
]
Skywalker not only passed his final test, but also inspired Vader to turn away from the dark side, destroying the
Sith
and fulfilling his destiny as the
Chosen One
.
[
25
]
Revival
[
]
"
Although I had been able to train with two of the greatest Jedi Masters who ever lived, my lessons were learned in unorthodox ways while in the heat of battle against the Empire. When I began my own temple, I hoped to offer a more traditional path into the Jedi Order for my students.
"
―Luke Skywalker
[
26
]
With the Sith defeated, Luke Skywalker wandered
the galaxy
searching for knowledge that could help restore the Jedi Order.
[
27
]
He trained his sister
Leia Organa
in the ways of the Force until she ceased her training to protect her son,
Ben Solo
, from the death she had foreseen would come if she continued down that path.
[
28
]
Skywalker went onto train the former Jedi youngling
Grogu
on
[
29
]
the planet
Ossus
,
[
30
]
where he used
ant droids
to contruct a new
Jedi school
. Grogu soon left the temple to reunite with his guardian
Din Djarin
[
29
]
and take up the life of a
Mandalorian
.
[
31
]
Rey tests out her skills with a lightsaber as Luke Skywalker looks on.
In addition to a dozen other students, Skywalker trained his nephew Ben in the Jedi arts
[
32
]
on Ossus.
[
30
]
Like his uncle and grandfather, Solo was naturally gifted in the ways of the Force, possessing raw strength
[
32
]
and the potential for limitless power.
[
33
]
As Ben
[
30
]
and the other initial students
[
34
]
grew older, new younglings came into the temple's care, with individuals like
Enyo
and the now-Padawan Solo helping Skywalker in passing down the Order's teachings.
[
30
]
It was in Solo that Skywalker placed his hopes for the future of the Jedi Order, and for a time he believed that he would not die as the last Jedi.
[
32
]
However, Skywalker's plans fell to ruin due to the machinations of
[
33
]
the secretly still-alive Palpatine
[
35
]
and his puppet,
[
28
]
Supreme Leader
Snoke
, who converted Solo to the dark side, which resulted in the
destruction
of Skywalker's new generation of Jedi.
[
33
]
Rejecting his mandate to rebuild the Jedi Order, Skywalker resolved to die in
exile
as the last of the Jedi, until he was located on
Ahch-To
by
Rey
, a
scavenger
from
Jakku
. Yet Skywalker had no intention of leaving his hermitage to help the
Resistance
in its
war
against the
First Order
, much less training another generation of Jedi. Rey's persistence eventually wore Skywalker down, however. Condensing Jedi training into three lessons, he agreed to teach Rey the ways of the Force. After Skywalker gave his life defending the Resistance during the
Battle of Crait
,
[
32
]
Rey resumed her training under Organa.
